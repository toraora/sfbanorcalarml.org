Welcome back! Here is what's new this year and what's coming up.

## New: SFBA Passport

We're launching [SFBA Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org), a
check-in site for local in-person math contests. Sign in with Discord, then
check in from your phone while you're at any of the partner events below.
Each check-in is worth a $6 discount on your ARML registration fee this spring,
up to $30 total.

Passport also keeps the list of upcoming Bay Area contests with dates,
locations, and eligibility. If you run a local in-person contest that isn't
listed, [tell us about it](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org/suggest).

## Discord

Announcements and season logistics (tryouts, rosters, travel) happen on the
[SFBA / NorCal ARML Discord server](https://discord.gg/XsqPrShvyR). Join if
you haven't already.

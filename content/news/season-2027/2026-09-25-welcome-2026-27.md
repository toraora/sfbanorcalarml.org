Welcome to the 2026–27 academic year! Here is what's new and what's coming up.

## New: SFBA Passport

We're partnering with local organizations and encouraging everyone to attend
and support their events. Each event participation gets you $6 off your ARML
registration fee, up to $30, and some of these events may also offer further
discounts as prizes for top students. Check
[SFBA Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org) to learn more.

If you are organizing a local-first, in-person event that is not already
listed, [tell us about it](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org/suggest)!

## Upcoming events

### Cowconuts Math Competition

**Sunday, September 27, 2026 · Amador Valley High School, Pleasanton · middle and high school students**

Individual and Guts rounds in two divisions: Division A at AMC 10 to AIME level
and Division B at AMC 8 to AMC 10 level. Teams of up to 4, with a suggested $10
donation. [Event site](https://cowconuts.org/2026/08/06/2026-9th-annual-cowconuts-math-competition/)
· [Check in on Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org/events/c363f8ad-bb53-47f9-a565-e9bcfa50272c/check-in)

### Stanford Mini Math Meet (SM3)

**Saturday, November 7, 2026 · Stanford University · middle school students**

Two team rounds in the morning (Hidden Gems, a problem-solving card game, and
Bingo, a 7 by 7 board of problems), followed by afternoon activities. Teams of
4 at $40 per team; registration opens September 28.
[Event site](https://www.stanfordmathtournament.org/competitions/sm3-2026)
· [Check in on Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org/events/d09b6a8a-d10e-4a67-8828-5805ee6d5618/check-in)

### Berkeley Math Tournament (BMT)

**Saturday, November 14, 2026 · UC Berkeley · middle and high school students**

Power, Individual, and Guts rounds. Teams of up to 6, registered through
ContestDojo at $15 per student.
[Event site](https://berkeley.mt/events/bmt-2026/)
· [Check in on Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org/events/1f7dc351-ac36-49aa-a58b-3b09f08489a0/check-in)

Welcome to the 2026–27 academic year! Here is what's new and what's coming up.

## New: SFBA Passport

We're partnering with local organizations and encouraging everyone to attend
and support their events. Each event participation gets you $6 off your ARML
registration fee, up to $30, and some of these events may also offer further
discounts as prizes for top students. Check
[SFBA Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org) to learn more.

If you are organizing a local-first, in-person event that is not already
listed, [tell us about it](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org/suggest)!

## Upcoming events

### Cowconuts Math Competition

**Sunday, September 27, 2026 · Amador Valley High School, Pleasanton**

The Bay Area Cowconuts Math Club's 9th annual contest is back in person this
year. Everyone takes an Individual Round and a Guts Round, with tiebreakers as
needed, and all of the problems are written by the club. There are two
divisions: Division A (AMC 10 to AIME level, grade 12 and below) and Division B
(AMC 8 to AMC 10 level, grade 9 and below). Register as a team of up to 4 by
September 18; the suggested donation is $10. While students compete, Po-Shen Loh
will give a talk for parents.

Open to middle and high school students · [Event site](https://cowconuts.org/2026/08/06/2026-9th-annual-cowconuts-math-competition/) · [Check in on Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org/events/c363f8ad-bb53-47f9-a565-e9bcfa50272c/check-in)

### Stanford Mini Math Meet (SM3)

**Saturday, November 7, 2026 · Stanford University**

SM3 is the Stanford Math Tournament's event for younger students with little or
no contest experience. In the morning, teams play Hidden Gems, a card game that
mixes problem solving with strategy, and Bingo, a race to complete rows on a
7×7 board of problems. After lunch there are activities or a puzzlehunt across
campus. Teams of exactly 4, $40 per team with financial aid available. Open
registration starts September 28 at 10am and is first come, first served.

Open to middle school students · [Event site](https://www.stanfordmathtournament.org/competitions/sm3-2026) · [Check in on Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org/events/d09b6a8a-d10e-4a67-8828-5805ee6d5618/check-in)

### Berkeley Math Tournament (BMT)

**Saturday, November 14, 2026 · UC Berkeley**

BMT returns to campus with its usual three rounds: Power, Individual, and Guts.
It's open to anyone in grade 12 or below, with no lower age limit, so advanced
middle schoolers are welcome. Teams have up to 6 students and register through
ContestDojo at $15 per student through October 18 ($25 after). If you can't make
it in person, BMT 2026 Online runs on December 5.

Open to middle and high school students · [Event site](https://berkeley.mt/events/bmt-2026/) · [Check in on Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org/events/1f7dc351-ac36-49aa-a58b-3b09f08489a0/check-in)

The full list of events is on [SFBA Passport](https://passport.sfbanorcalarml.org).
